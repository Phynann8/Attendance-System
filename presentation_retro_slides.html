01. THE CORE PROBLEM & MOTIVATION

Why Traditional Paper / Chat-Based Attendance Breaks Down

DISCONNECTED CHANNELS
1. Communication Gap
Parents notify front desk or message in chat groups; homeroom teachers don't get the message before class, causing false truancy alerts.
SUBJECTIVE MARKING
2. Inaccurate Late Minutes
Teachers guess arrival time when busy teaching. Gate arrivals aren't synced with class start timestamps.
LACK OF AUDIT
3. Disputed Status Changes
When an absence is changed to excused later, there is no verifiable log of who authorized the change, when, or why.
ROLE CONFUSION
4. Role Segregation Failure
Teachers shouldn't have to call parents during class time; Student Affairs and Admin should handle verification workflows.
💡 SOLUTION: A unified state machine with 4 segregated roles, strict 15-rule validation logic, and timestamp-based automated late calculations.

04. THE FROZEN BUSINESS WORKFLOW

15 Strict Business Rules Driving the State Transition Engine

1
Parent Request & Admin Pre-Approval RULES 9 & 10
Parent submits leave before class. Admin approves. When teacher opens roll-call, that student's row is 🔒 PRE-LOCKED.
2
Teacher 2-State Marking & Submission RULES 1 & 2
Teacher only marks Present or Absent (never late). Submitting finalizes teacher phase & unlocks Student Affairs queue.
3
Student Affairs Gate Verification RULES 3 & 4
If student arrives at gate ➔ SA clicks 'Mark Arrived'. System calculates exact minutes_late from submit time ➔ Status: LATE (Case Closed).
4
Admin Adjudication & Audit Trail RULES 13, 14 & 15
If student never arrived ➔ SA escalates to Admin. Valid parent reason = EXCUSED. Invalid = ABSENT WITHOUT PERMISSION. Audit logged!
🎯 FOUR FINAL OUTCOMES: Present · Late (X min) · Excused · Absent Without Permission
